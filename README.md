I build and operate revenue-critical backend systems. At [OmniChat](https://omni.chat), Latin America's largest conversational-commerce SaaS, I own the billing, plans and contracts platform: 8+ Node.js/TypeScript microservices on AWS serverless (Lambda, SQS, EventBridge).

- **Systems that handle money.** Architected and led a zero-downtime rewrite of the company's billing platform: event-keyed idempotency, dynamic parallelism, automated reconciliation and per-stage observability. It ended years of recurring duplicate charges and invoice failures.
- **Quality as an engineering discipline.** Golden-master E2E suites with 100% branch and mutation coverage, phased coverage programs and CI quality gates for money-path code.
- **AI-first workflow.** I run a custom agentic development harness (Claude Code, MCP servers, custom skills and plugins, parallel worker agents) that multiplies delivery throughput while keeping quality gates strict.
- **Product mindset.** Former product manager and full-stack developer; I care about the business outcome, not just the ticket.
- **Always learning.** Master's in Artificial Intelligence (AGTU, USA, English-language program) and Full Cycle 4.0 in progress. US-educated (high school and university in Georgia, USA).
